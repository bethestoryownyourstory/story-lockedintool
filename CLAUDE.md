# STORY - working rules

- **STORY works as one with the phone.** Once on, it is on and always running, like part of the system, and it uses the phone's full potential: STORY does things itself (e.g. silencing apps under the Pages) instead of depending on other apps to cooperate.
- **Black means real black (#000000).** Whenever the owner says "black", use pure #000 -- never a dark grey. The Pages background and cards are #000 (html.focus).
- **Change only what the owner asks for.** Every design in STORY stays exactly as it is unless the owner says to change it - no "improvements", no side changes. If the owner says something is wrong but not where or how it should be, ASK before changing anything. Only fix directly when it's clear what is wrong and how it should look.

- All source files live in the repo root, flat (no folders). `.github/workflows/android.yml` turns them into an Android project at build time; `android.yml` / `screens.yml` / `deploy-worker.yml` in the root are copies of the workflows - keep them identical.
- Fixes to STORY go in these real app files, committed and pushed **straight to `main`**.
- After every push, confirm "Build STORY APK" is green. It fails unless the APK is signed with the repo key (`debug.keystore.b64`, SHA-256 FC58...3569) and its versionCode (run number + 100) is higher than the published one. Only `main` builds are published to phones.
- Updates run for everyone even when STORY is off (WorkManager `UpdateWorker`, every 15 min) and are shown on the setup screen ("Updates: ..."). STORY stays on once started until "Stop STORY" (boot, update, accessibility connect and the worker all restart it).
- **Station 3 is LOCKED (owner's final word).** The corner button sits tight in the bottom-right corner: 40x40, solid pure black #000000 with NO outline (owner: "black that"), rounded top-left, up-left arrow. It never moves, resizes, changes look or animates, open or closed; opening only adds the white bar (40px tall) to its left. No animation anywhere in Station 3 or the dial pad. The dial pad is its own still window (`?s3=1&dial=1`, `dialPane`) in the middle above Station 3, shown/hidden only; Station 3's window is sized once while hidden and never resizes on screen. Never change its position, size or look - and never let another change shift it - unless the owner explicitly asks in their own words. There is ONE Station 3 button: the app's own (bubble in OverlayService.kt), always on screen and on top, open or closed, over apps AND over the Pages (the Pages page draws no Station 3 of its own: `html.native:not(.s3-early) .station3-region` is hidden); tap toggles Station 3. The page never draws a visible button (invisible placeholder only) and the white bar runs on underneath the button (never cut). The bar's white is drawn by the app (barBg in OverlayService.kt, same 40dp maths as the button) so its height matches the button to the pixel; the page draws only the icons on it (html.s3-native-bar). Windows use pinToScreen() so Android can't shift them; the s3-only CSS/JS in index.html and the bubble/pane code in OverlayService.kt hold this in place.
- Always On Display (AodActivity, switch "Always On Display" on the setup screen, off by default): on screen-off a black always-on screen shows at the phone's own brightness (no override - owner's call); Station 3's overlay windows stay on top, unchanged and working. Burn-in protection = a 1-pixel checkerboard over Station 3's windows that swaps every minute (applyAodMask) - Station 3 never moves. Double-tap = lock screen, power = truly off.
- Two channels. Test = `latest` release + gh-pages `/preview/` (the owner's phone, owner mode). Live = `live` release + gh-pages `/` (all users). Both update automatically, no confirmations.
- Using STORY = agreeing to automatic updates (owner's rule, everyone incl. the owner). UpdateActivity is the one-time "Automatic updates - I agree" screen; until agreed STORY stays off (OverlayService/KeepAlive gate on `Release.agreedAuto`). After agreeing, never asked or reminded again. Checks every 20s while the screen is on.
- Nothing goes Live until the owner publishes: the "Publish live update" button (PIN checked by the Cloudflare Worker in `release-worker.js`) or the owner saying "approve" in chat - then run "STORY screens - Test, then Approve". Never put the PIN in the app or the repo.
- The release server address lives in `release.json`. "Deploy STORY release server" puts `release-worker.js` on Cloudflare (needs repo secrets CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN and CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID).
